Glossary of signals
What each of the 12 signals is actually measuring.
Animal safetyPhysical or chemical hazard risk to the pet
Human safetyHazard risk to the humans handling or near it
Health & wellbeingSupport for the pet's physical health (posture, warmth, pressure relief)
ComfortHow pleasant/soft the product feels to the pet
CleanlinessHow well it resists dirt, odor, or staining over time
Easy maintenanceHow easy it is to wash, clean, or upkeep
ConvenienceEase of use, storage, and handling for the owner
InteractionHow it facilitates engagement between pet and owner
AgencyHow much freedom/control the pet has (movement, choice)
OrganisationHow well it helps keep the home tidy
Space efficiencyHow compact or space-saving the product is
AestheticsVisual style and appeal
The Gate & DNA panel groups some of these into composites: Pet satisfaction (health & wellbeing + comfort + a share of interaction), Human satisfaction (convenience + agency + a share of interaction), and Orderliness (organisation + space efficiency). Animal safety, human safety, cleanliness, and aesthetics stand alone.
Glossary of assessment results
What each final Selection Engine result means. The Gate is a hard constraint and can override the Selection Score band.
SELECTGate passed and Selection Score is ≥80. The product is ready to be selected under the current evidence and configuration.
TESTGate passed and Selection Score is ≥65 but below 80, or a raw SELECT result is capped at TEST because animal/human safety evidence is incomplete or below its floor.
HOLDThe product is not ready for a selection decision because the Gate requires more evidence, such as insufficient coverage, low confidence, or partial DNA. A score band of 50–64.9 also maps to HOLD when the Gate passes.
REJECTA hard Gate condition failed, such as a critical safety hazard or failed DNA threshold, or the Selection Score is below 50 when the Gate passes.
Important: Product assessment and transaction economics are separate decisions. A pricing result such as REVIEW or NOT SUPPORTED does not by itself change the product assessment result.
Glossary of Gate & DNA dimensions
What the Gate checks, what each DNA dimension measures, and the configured floors used by the Selection Engine.

Gate

Gate statusPASS, HOLD, or REJECT. The Gate is a hard constraint and overrides the Selection Score band.
Hard safety triggerREJECT if animal or human safety evidence is strongly negative (polarity < 0 and strength ≥ 3), or if verified injury, manufacturer hazard warning, or structural failure hazard is present.
Evidence sufficiencyHOLD when signal coverage is below the limited-evidence threshold or aggregate confidence is below 40%.
DNA thresholdsNon-safety DNA dimensions are checked against their configured floors. A missing dimension places the Gate on HOLD; a dimension below its floor fails the Gate.
Safety verificationHuman and animal safety dimensions are tracked as safety unverified when evidence is present but the dimension remains below its floor. This does not itself fail the Gate, but it can cap a SELECT decision at TEST.
Overall DNAHOLD when Overall DNA is missing or partial; REJECT only when complete Overall DNA is below the configured overall floor.

DNA dimensions

Human safetyDirectly reflects the Human safety signal. Floor: 80.
Animal safetyDirectly reflects the Animal safety signal. Floor: 85.
CleanlinessDirectly reflects the Cleanliness signal. Floor: 60.
AestheticsDirectly reflects the Aesthetics signal. Floor: 50.
Pet satisfactionComposite of Health & wellbeing (34%), Comfort (33%), and an Interaction share (33%). Interaction contributes half of its normalized score to this dimension. Floor: 50.
Human satisfactionComposite of Convenience (34%), Agency (33%), and an Interaction share (33%). Interaction contributes half of its normalized score to this dimension. Floor: 50.
OrderlinessComposite of Organisation (50%) and Space efficiency (50%). Floor: 55.
Overall DNA (AD)Weighted composite of the DNA dimensions, using the configured weights and renormalising over dimensions that have values. Floor: 70.
